## Owner-reported creation process

Vitalii Kolosov reports that the project's main icons and logo were created with **ChatGPT Images (OpenAI)** from his visual requirements, then selected and iteratively adjusted at his direction for use in the application. This statement supplies provenance previously missing from the audit; it is recorded as owner-reported evidence, not an independent determination of exclusive copyright or trademark clearance.

The three branding master sets below are correlated with that statement through the existing icon/brand records and OpenAI/ChatGPT/C2PA markers observed in the earlier audit. The file-to-family mapping is supported by those records and a focused catalog/hash check; no creation-session or C2PA-signature validation is claimed. If the owner knows of an exception, an external reference/input, or a different source for a particular set, that exception should be recorded separately. Other PNGs are not assigned ChatGPT provenance merely because they are in this repository.

## OpenAI output rights and their limits

The [Europe Terms of Use, Content section](https://openai.com/policies/eu-terms-of-use/) and, where applicable, [Services Agreement, sections 4.1–4.4](https://openai.com/policies/services-agreement/) allocate OpenAI's rights, if any, in output to its customer subject to applicable law. The relevant terms depend on the service, region and account used. On that basis, **using ChatGPT Images does not itself require buying a separate image-redistribution license from OpenAI**; this does not waive service charges or other applicable obligations. This is an inference from those output-rights provisions, not individualized contract clearance.

That allocation does not guarantee uniqueness, exclusive copyright, non-infringement or absence of third-party resemblance, and it does not clear rights to external inputs. Copyrightability of AI-generated elements and the effect of human creative contributions depend on applicable law. No determination of copyright registration or trademark availability has been made. Owner-reported generation is not a reason either to declare the whole design infringing or to claim exclusive protection over every pixel.

Official terms and service-specific provisions were consulted on 2026-11-12; the [Service Terms](https://openai.com/policies/service-terms/) are part of that framework. The owner confirms rights in this personal project and no employer contractual restriction. Private contracts/account records and historical terms governing a particular generation were not independently examined. This owner confirmation does not replace third-party permissions or prove exclusive rights in AI output. No source artwork was uploaded to an external verification service in this follow-up.
