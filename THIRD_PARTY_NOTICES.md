## Packaged notices and security follow-up

`MyApp/Resources/ThirdPartyNotices.txt` and `MyApp/CodeMarkupCore/Resources/code-markup-licenses.txt` accompany the app. `python3 scripts/check-third-party-notices.py [path/to/ToolHold.app]` checks preservation of all retained license/notice texts, including OpenSSL and the project notice. This does not replace legal review or provenance verification. Existing upstream notifications have not been removed.

The [OpenSSL advisory list](https://openssl-library.org/news/vulnerabilities/) was checked on 2026-11-12. Five low-severity advisories dated 2026-11-10 cover versions before 4.0.4, including the pinned 4.0.3. Source review found no application use of AES-GCM-SIV, CMS encryption or QUIC (the last is disabled in the build). This is a reachability assessment, not a promise of zero exposure. A reviewed update to a current stable release, all-slice rebuild and crypto regression testing should precede binary distribution; no crypto dependency upgrade was attempted as an incidental documentation change. Dated npm audit snapshots are historical evidence and were not presented as a current all-dependency vulnerability scan.
