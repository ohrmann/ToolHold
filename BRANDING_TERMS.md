## 3. Branding boundaries and unofficial modified builds

This permission does not authorize:

- Using the covered logos or artwork as the branding of an unrelated product.
- Selling the covered artwork as standalone graphics or using it for merchandising.
- Presenting an unofficial modified build as an official ToolHold release.
- Suggesting that Vitalii Kolosov approves, supports, sponsors or endorses a third-party product or distributor without separate authorization.

Copying raw image files within a qualifying source repository or technical fork is an allowed use under section 2, not a prohibited standalone sale of graphics. Inclusion of the artwork in a permitted application distribution is likewise distinct from selling the artwork itself.

**Publicly distributed modified builds must be clearly identified as unofficial.** A modified build here includes changes to project source or content; ordinary compilation, signing, packaging and the technical image adaptations allowed in section 2 alone do not make an otherwise unchanged copy a modified build. An unchanged third-party build must still not be falsely presented as an official release.

For a modified build, place a prominent statement such as **“Unofficial ToolHold build — modified by [distributor]”**, or an equally clear translation, at the download/release location and in accompanying distribution documentation. Keep that identification with the distribution. If its presentation would otherwise mislead users about official origin or endorsement, correct that presentation as well. This does not require changing the application name or icons merely because a repository is a technical fork. Local-only modifications do not require a public-distribution label.

References to names or marks are permitted only as reasonably needed for the allowed identification and artwork uses above. No broader permission to brand unrelated products, or license to another person's trademark, is granted. Lawful factual references and rights that do not require this grant remain unaffected.
